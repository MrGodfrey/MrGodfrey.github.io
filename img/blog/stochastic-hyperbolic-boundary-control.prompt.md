## Design brief

A top-down two-dimensional rectangular domain contains two coupled transport
fields: blue moving horizontally right, teal moving diagonally up and right.
The left and bottom edges highlight incoming control channels in amber.
Noise marks and amber points represent random forcing and control of the random
term throughout the interior. A time arrow connects this schematic to a second
domain with both components zero everywhere at the final time. This is an
illustration, not a computed trajectory or a claim of boundary-only control.
